# MealMatch
For students, busy adults, and families who struggle to decide what to cook while trying to save time and money, the MealMatch app is a meal-planning and recipe-suggestion tool that provides quick, practical meal ideas based on the user’s existing ingredients, budget, and available cooking time. MealMatch aims to encourage users to have a reasonable plan before
buying groceries to reduce food waste and the frequency of eating out. Different from apps like
SuperCook, Mealime, Yummly, and Paprika, the product focuses on straightforward meal
suggestions that match what the user already has at home, instead of making them search
through an overwhelming number of recipes. By simplifying its users’ decision-making around
meal time, MealMatch helps users feel more confident and organized about their household
spending.
Through user questions and market research, the most common challenges surrounding
meal planning are used to construct this product vision. Because many people struggle deciding
what to cook, food waste has become a widespread issue. With grocery prices rising and time
and money becoming more limited, users need extra support to make the most of the food they
already have. Other meal-planning apps already exist, indicating strong market interest in
similar applications; MealMach wants to solve the difficult question of “What can I make right
now?” To help validate the needs and desires MealMatch can address, users can be asked
questions regarding their lifestyle, such as “How often do you cook at home?” or “What makes
meal planning difficult?” Direct user insight and feedback will help create a more focused and
direct solution over time.
